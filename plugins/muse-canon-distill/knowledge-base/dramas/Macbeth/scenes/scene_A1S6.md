## **Act 1 Scene 6**

*running scene 6*

*Hautboys and Torches. Enter King* \[*Duncan*\]*, Malcolm, Donalbain*, *Banquo, Lennox, Macduff, Ross, Angus and Attendants*

**DUNCAN**    This castle hath a pleasant seat1: the air

Nimbly and sweetly recommends itself

Unto our gentle3 senses.

**BANQUO**    This guest of summer,

The temple-haunting martlet, does approve5

By his loved mansionry6 that the heaven’s breath

Smells wooingly here: no jutty, frieze7,

Buttress, nor coign of vantage8, but this bird

Hath made his pendent bed and procreant9 cradle:

Where they most breed and haunt, I have observed

The air is delicate.11

*Enter Lady* \[*Macbeth*\]

**DUNCAN**    See, see, our honoured hostess.—

The love that follows us sometime is our trouble13,

Which still we thank as love. Herein I teach you14

How you shall bid God yield15 us for your pains,

And thank us for your trouble.

**LADY MACBETH**    All our service

In every point18 twice done, and then done double

Were poor and single business to contend19

Against those honours deep and broad wherewith

Your majesty loads our house: for those of old21,

And the late22 dignities heaped up to them,

We rest your hermits.23

**DUNCAN**    Where’s the Thane of Cawdor?

We coursed25 him at the heels, and had a purpose

To be his purveyor26: but he rides well,

And his great love, sharp as his spur, hath holp27 him

To his home before us. Fair and noble hostess,

We are your guest tonight.

**LADY MACBETH**    Your servants ever

Have theirs, themselves and what is theirs, in compt31

To make their audit32 at your highness’ pleasure,

Still to return your own.33

**DUNCAN**    Give me your hand,

Conduct me to mine host: we love him highly,

And shall continue our graces36 towards him.

By your leave37, hostess.

*Exeunt*
